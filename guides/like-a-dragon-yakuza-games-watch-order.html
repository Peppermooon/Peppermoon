Like a Dragon / Yakuza Watch Order

Trace Kiryu’s saga, move into Ichiban’s games and choose the remakes.

Like a Dragon, formerly known internationally as Yakuza, follows Kazuma Kiryu across decades before introducing Ichiban Kasuga as a new lead. The remakes let newcomers use a newer version of the first three adventures. They do not create extra chapters to play between the originals.

Choose order

Release order: main saga and connected spin-offs

  1. Yakuza (2005 • Game)
  2. Yakuza 2 (2006 • Game)
  3. Yakuza 3 (2009 • Game)
  4. Yakuza 4 (2010 • Game)
  5. Yakuza: Dead Souls (2011 • Optional alternate-story Game)
  6. Yakuza 5 (2012 • Game)
  7. Yakuza 0 (2015 • Game prequel)
  8. Yakuza Kiwami (2016 • Remake Game of Yakuza)
  9. Yakuza 6: The Song of Life (2016 • Game)
  10. Yakuza Kiwami 2 (2017 • Remake Game of Yakuza 2)
  11. Judgment (2018 • Detective Game spin-off)
  12. Yakuza: Like a Dragon (2020 • Game; also called Like a Dragon in some regions)
  13. Lost Judgment (2021 • Detective Game sequel)
  14. Like a Dragon: Ishin! (2023 • Remake Game of a 2014 historical spin-off)
  15. Like a Dragon Gaiden: The Man Who Erased His Name (2023 • Game)
  16. Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth (2024 • Game)
  17. Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii (2025 • Game)
  18. Yakuza Kiwami 3 & Dark Ties (2026 • Remake Game plus new side-story campaign)

Release years here refer to the games’ first release in Japan. Some titles arrived in other regions later. Yakuza 3, 4 and 5 also had remastered releases; those versions do not change their place in the story.

Story order: Kiryu’s saga, then Ichiban’s

  1. Yakuza 0 (2015 • Game; 1988).
  2. Yakuza (2005 • Game; 1995 and 2005) or Yakuza Kiwami (2016 • Remake Game; same main story).
  3. Yakuza 2 (2006 • Game; 2006) or Yakuza Kiwami 2 (2017 • Remake Game; same main story).
  4. Yakuza 3 (2009 • Game; 2009) or Yakuza Kiwami 3 (2026 • Remake Game; same core chapter).
  5. Yakuza 4 (2010 • Game; 2010).
  6. Yakuza 5 (2012 • Game; 2012).
  7. Yakuza 6: The Song of Life (2016 • Game; 2016).
  8. Judgment (2018 • Detective Game; 2018; optional side story).
  9. Yakuza: Like a Dragon (2020 • Game; 2019–2020).
  10. Lost Judgment (2021 • Detective Game; 2021; optional side story).
  11. Like a Dragon Gaiden (2023 • Game; overlaps Like a Dragon and bridges into Infinite Wealth).
  12. Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth (2024 • Game; 2023–2024).
  13. Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii (2025 • Game; after Infinite Wealth).

Optional historical stories: Like a Dragon: Ishin! (2023 • Remake Game; 1860s) is a separate period drama with familiar actors and names in a historical setting, not a chapter in Kiryu’s modern story. Dead Souls (2011 • Game) is an alternate zombie spin-off. Dark Ties (2026 • Side-story Game campaign) explores Yoshitaka Mine before the events of Yakuza 3; for context, play it after reaching the third chapter.

Should I play Yakuza 0 first?

For story chronology, yes. For a first-time player who wants to experience the series as it developed, Yakuza Kiwami is also a strong start, followed by Kiwami 2 and then the later games. Kiwami includes additions that assume familiarity with Yakuza 0, so either beginning works with that caveat.

Peppermoon’s recommendation

Choose the Kiwami remakes for the first three Kiryu chapters if you want updated presentation. Continue with Yakuza 4, 5 and 6, then move into Ichiban’s games. Judgment is a good optional side path in the same modern setting.

Sources: SEGA’s Like a Dragon game catalogue; SEGA on Kiwami 3 & Dark Ties; SEGA studio history.
