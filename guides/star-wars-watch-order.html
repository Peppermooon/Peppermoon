Star Wars Watch Order

Follow the main canon story across the prequel, original and sequel eras, including animated and live-action series.

Star Wars gives you two very different ways into its galaxy. Start with the original films and you discover its mysteries alongside Luke Skywalker. Start earlier in the timeline and you watch the Republic’s decline before the rebellion begins. Both approaches work, but they reveal information in different ways.

Where do the anthology shows fit?

Tales of the Jedi, Tales of the Empire and Tales of the Underworld move between periods. They cannot honestly occupy one fixed position on a timeline. Watch Tales of the Jedi after the prequel trilogy and The Clone Wars; save the other Tales collections until you know the related characters from the main shows. Forces of Destiny (2017) also consists of short adventures spread across eras.

The Clone Wars itself was not originally broadcast in strict story order. For a detailed rewatch, use Lucasfilm’s official episode chronology. For a first watch, its normal episode order remains approachable.

Visions, LEGO productions and older Legends material such as the 2003 Clone Wars series are optional viewing outside this main canon route. They should not be inserted as if every production shares one history.

Peppermoon’s recommendation

Start with Episodes IV, V and VI, then I, II and III, followed by VII, VIII and IX. Add Rogue One and Andor for the rebellion, or The Mandalorian for a new adventure. You do not need to finish every animated series before enjoying your first Star Wars film.
