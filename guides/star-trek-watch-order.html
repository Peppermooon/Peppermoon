Star Trek Watch Order

Explore the Prime timeline, classic crews and the separate Kelvin film universe.

Star Trek is less a single marathon than a collection of crews exploring the same enormous future. Its shows often run alongside one another, and some jump centuries forward. The easiest approach is to choose a crew, follow its adventures, and then expand your journey.

Story order: the Prime timeline

  1. Enterprise — the 22nd century, before the familiar Federation era. [Released: 2001 • TV series]
  2. Discovery, seasons 1–2 — the 23rd century before Kirk’s main adventures. [Released: 2017 • TV series]
  3. Strange New Worlds — Captain Pike’s Enterprise. [Released: 2022 • TV series]
  4. The Original Series. [Released: 1966 • TV series]
  5. The Animated Series. [Released: 1973 • TV series]
  6. The Motion Picture. [Released: 1979 • Film]
  7. The Wrath of Khan. [Released: 1982 • Film]
  8. The Search for Spock. [Released: 1984 • Film]
  9. The Voyage Home. [Released: 1986 • Film]
  10. The Final Frontier. [Released: 1989 • Film]
  11. The Undiscovered Country. [Released: 1991 • Film]
  12. Section 31 — the early 24th century. Its protagonist’s personal journey also depends on Discovery, including season 3. [Released: 2025 • Film]
  13. The Next Generation — begins in 2364. [Released: 1987 • TV series]
  14. Deep Space Nine — begins in 2369, overlapping The Next Generation’s final seasons. [Released: 1993 • TV series]
  15. Generations — its main story follows The Next Generation finale. [Released: 1994 • Film]
  16. Voyager — begins in 2371, overlapping Deep Space Nine. [Released: 1995 • TV series]
  17. First Contact, then Insurrection — during the wider Deep Space Nine/Voyager period. [Released: 1996 • Film; 1998 • Film]
  18. Nemesis — after Voyager’s main television story. [Released: 2002 • Film]
  19. Lower Decks. [Released: 2020 • TV series]
  20. Prodigy. [Released: 2021 • TV series]
  21. Picard. [Released: 2020 • TV series]
  22. Discovery, seasons 3–5 — the 32nd century, with an epilogue later still. [Released: 2017 • TV series]
  23. Starfleet Academy — the 32nd-century setting following Discovery’s main late-era story, not necessarily after its distant epilogue. [Released: 2026 • TV series]

This is an era-based route, not a claim that all of Deep Space Nine happens before Voyager. You can watch those shows separately without constantly switching episodes. Short Treks spans different periods; use it as companion viewing after meeting the relevant crews.

Story order: the Kelvin timeline, a separate three-film route

Watch Star Trek (2009) → Into Darkness (2013) → Beyond (2016). Their main adventures take place in an alternate 23rd century. They do not replace the events of The Original Series in the Prime timeline.

Peppermoon’s recommendation

For a modern introduction, start with Strange New Worlds. For the classic experience, start with The Original Series or The Next Generation. Save crossover-heavy Picard and the reference-rich Lower Decks until you have some familiarity with the earlier crews.
