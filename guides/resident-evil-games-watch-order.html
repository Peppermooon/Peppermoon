EVERY UNIVERSE. IN ORDER.

Resident Evil Watch Order

Follow the outbreak saga with remakes identified as alternate versions.

Resident Evil combines outbreak stories, recurring characters and several remakes. A remake retells an older game’s place in the story, so its modern release year does not move the events forward. This guide covers the main saga and the two Revelations side stories.

Choose order

Release order

  1. Resident Evil (1996 • Game)
  2. Resident Evil 2 (1998 • Game)
  3. Resident Evil 3: Nemesis (1999 • Game)
  4. Resident Evil Code: Veronica (2000 • Game)
  5. Resident Evil 0 (2002 • Game prequel)
  6. Resident Evil (2002 • Remake Game)
  7. Resident Evil 4 (2005 • Game)
  8. Resident Evil 5 (2009 • Game)
  9. Resident Evil: Revelations (2012 • Game)
  10. Resident Evil 6 (2012 • Game)
  11. Resident Evil: Revelations 2 (2015 • Game)
  12. Resident Evil 7: Biohazard (2017 • Game)
  13. Resident Evil 2 (2019 • Remake Game)
  14. Resident Evil 3 (2020 • Remake Game)
  15. Resident Evil Village (2021 • Game)
  16. Resident Evil 4 (2023 • Remake Game)
  17. Resident Evil Requiem (2026 • Game)

The 2019, 2020 and 2023 remakes are separate releases that retell their originals. Pick the original or remake for each chapter; there is no need to play both to understand the main plot. Code: Veronica remains a released 2000 game. Its announced remake is not included as a released game.

Story order

  1. Resident Evil 0 (2002 • Game; July 1998).
  2. Resident Evil (1996 • Game) or Resident Evil (2002 • Remake Game; July 1998).
  3. Resident Evil 2 (1998 • Game) or Resident Evil 2 (2019 • Remake Game; September 1998).
  4. Resident Evil 3: Nemesis (1999 • Game) or Resident Evil 3 (2020 • Remake Game; September–October 1998). Its events overlap Resident Evil 2; the order here avoids splitting games into sections.
  5. Resident Evil Code: Veronica (2000 • Game; December 1998).
  6. Resident Evil 4 (2005 • Game) or Resident Evil 4 (2023 • Remake Game; 2004).
  7. Resident Evil: Revelations (2012 • Game; 2005).
  8. Resident Evil 5 (2009 • Game; 2009).
  9. Resident Evil: Revelations 2 (2015 • Game; its story spans 2011 and 2013).
  10. Resident Evil 6 (2012 • Game; 2012–2013).
  11. Resident Evil 7: Biohazard (2017 • Game; 2017).
  12. Resident Evil Village (2021 • Game; 2021).
  13. Shadows of Rose (2022 • Story DLC Game; 2037).
  14. Resident Evil Requiem (2026 • Game; later than Village, set in 2028).

Peppermoon’s recommendation

For a modern first play, use the remakes of Resident Evil, 2, 3 and 4, then continue with 5, 6, 7, Village and Requiem. Add Code: Veronica and Revelations when you want the wider character story. If you prefer seeing how the series developed, use release order.

Sources: Capcom’s Resident Evil history and timeline; Capcom’s Requiem portal; Capcom 30th anniversary.
