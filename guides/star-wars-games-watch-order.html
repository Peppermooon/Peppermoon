Star Wars Games Watch Order

Separate the current canon adventures from Legends classics such as KOTOR.

Star Wars games cover several continuities. Some belong to current screen canon; other celebrated adventures, including Knights of the Old Republic, are Legends stories. This guide focuses on narrative console and PC games rather than every LEGO, mobile, arcade and multiplayer release.

Choose order

Release order: selected story-led games

  1. Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic (2003 • Game; Legends RPG)
  2. Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II — The Sith Lords (2004 • Game; Legends RPG)
  3. Star Wars: Republic Commando (2005 • Game; Legends Shooter)
  4. Star Wars: The Force Unleashed (2008 • Game; Legends Action-Adventure)
  5. Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II (2010 • Game; Legends Action-Adventure)
  6. Star Wars Battlefront II (2017 • Game; Canon Campaign Shooter)
  7. Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order (2019 • Game; Canon Action-Adventure)
  8. Star Wars: Squadrons (2020 • Game; Canon Flight Combat)
  9. Star Wars Jedi: Survivor (2023 • Game; Canon Action-Adventure)
  10. Star Wars Outlaws (2024 • Game; Canon Open-World Adventure)

Story order: selected connected timelines

Legends, Old Republic era:

  1. Knights of the Old Republic (2003 • Game; Legends RPG; about 4,000 years before the films).
  2. Knights of the Old Republic II (2004 • Game; Legends RPG; follows the first game).

These games are not part of the current canon timeline. Republic Commando and The Force Unleashed games are also Legends and belong to separate story arcs; play them in release order.

Current canon, around the Empire and Rebellion:

  1. Jedi: Fallen Order (2019 • Canon Game; about 14 years before A New Hope).
  2. Jedi: Survivor (2023 • Canon Game; five years after Fallen Order).
  3. Outlaws (2024 • Canon Game; between The Empire Strikes Back and Return of the Jedi).
  4. Battlefront II campaign (2017 • Canon Game; begins around Return of the Jedi and spans into the sequel era).
  5. Squadrons (2020 • Canon Game; after Return of the Jedi).

This is an era guide, not a perfect day-by-day sequence. Battlefront II’s campaign jumps across several years, and Squadrons follows different pilots. The Jedi games form the most directly connected pair and should be played in order.

How does this connect with the Star Wars films and shows?

The games can enrich a screen marathon, but they are optional. Jedi: Fallen Order and Survivor sit during the Imperial era; Outlaws takes place between Episodes V and VI. KOTOR belongs to a separate Legends history, thousands of years before the films.

Peppermoon’s recommendation

Start with Jedi: Fallen Order, then Jedi: Survivor for a focused, story-driven introduction. Outlaws is a separate adventure with a new lead. Choose KOTOR if you want a classic role-playing game and are comfortable with it being outside the current film continuity.
